# Keep Life Assistant and Knowledge contexts isolated

Hermes Life Assistant and Hermes Knowledge may live in the same repository and share Hermes delivery infrastructure, including Agent Result Envelopes, but their first-version domain data and artifacts stay isolated. Life Assistant does not read or create Knowledge Files, Theme Views, Knowledge Cards, Review Items, Capture Drafts, or other Knowledge artifacts; Knowledge does not read Life Assistant lifestyle records, reminders, or reviews. This sacrifices some unified-assistant convenience, but keeps two independently evolving systems from coupling through ambiguous personal-state and knowledge-management semantics.
